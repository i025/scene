assemble(nodes, name='Assembly') groups shapes without merging their meshes.
fuse(*nodes), subtract(base,*cutters), overlap(*nodes), exclusive(left,right) construct solids.
node.move(offset), node.turn(axis, degrees, pivot=None), node.resize(factors, pivot=None)
modify a node and its descendants. node.turn(euler=(rx,ry,rz)) applies X, then Y, then Z.
Default rotation/scale pivot is the geometry's world-space bounding-box center.
shifted(node, offset), turned(node, axis, degrees, pivot=None, euler=None),
resized(node, factors, pivot=None) perform the same operations on independent copies.
node.paint(color, opacity=1) applies color to the subtree; node.rgba reads its finish.
node.centroid, node.dimensions, node.limits give AABB center, size, (minimum,maximum).
node.landmark('top'), node.landmark('front_left_bottom') return bounding-box anchors.
node.extreme(direction) returns a support point; projected_span(node,direction) returns bounds
along a unit direction; projected_width(node,direction) returns their difference.
snap(node, target, source_anchor='bottom', target_anchor='top', offset=(0,0,0)) returns a copy.
A target can also be a world point; then omit target_anchor.
abut(node, target, axis='+z', gap=0) places a copy after a target boundary.
center_on(node, target, axes=('x','y','z')) aligns selected center coordinates.
offset_at(node, reference, offsets) positions selected coordinates; None preserves a coordinate.
repeat_line(nodes, axis='+x', spacing=1) uses equal center spacing, keeping the first fixed.
